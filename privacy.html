Privacy Policy

World Mystery Studio

Effective Date: November 16, 2026

1. About This Policy

World Mystery Studio is a video creation and editing tool for historical storytelling. This policy describes how information is handled when using the studio and its optional third-party integrations.

2. Information We Process

The studio processes video projects, images, audio, subtitles, and editing settings provided by users.

If TikTok integration is enabled and authorized, the studio may process authorized account identifiers, access credentials, video metadata, and information needed for uploading content and checking its status.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to create, edit, preview, export, and manage videos. When authorized by the user, necessary information may also be used to transfer selected content to TikTok.

4. Storage and Security

Video projects are generally processed in the environment running the studio. Storage and security arrangements for connected accounts depend on the integration implementation. These arrangements will be reviewed before account integration is released.

5. Third-Party Services

If a user chooses to connect TikTok, selected information and content may be transmitted to TikTok to perform the authorized action. TikTok processes information according to its own privacy policies.

6. User Choices and Data Deletion

Users may choose not to connect third-party accounts. Users may request access to or deletion of personal information held by the service by contacting the operator.

Deletion requests will be reviewed and handled in accordance with applicable requirements. Information held by TikTok must be managed through TikTok's own controls.

7. Children's Privacy

The studio is not specifically directed at children. Users must comply with applicable age requirements.

8. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when new features or integrations are introduced. The effective date will be updated when changes are published.

9. Contact
